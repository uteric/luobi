Privacy Policy

Luobi (落笔) · Effective November 19, 2026

Luobi is a diary and notes app made by Yuanqing Ma (“we”). In short: we do not collect, store, sell, or see any of your data. Everything you write stays on your device and, if you choose, in your own iCloud or your own Google Drive.

Information we collect

None. Luobi has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. We never receive your diary entries, notes, photos, moods, tags, or any other content.

Where your content is stored

  • On your device. All entries, photos and settings are saved locally on your iPhone.
  • iCloud (optional, on by default). If you are signed in to iCloud, Luobi syncs your content across your own devices using Apple’s CloudKit private database. This data is stored in your iCloud account and is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy. We cannot access it. You can turn iCloud sync off in Luobi’s Settings.
  • Google Drive (optional, off by default). If you choose to connect Google Drive, Luobi uses Google Sign-In to request access to the drive.file scope, which only lets Luobi see and manage files it creates itself — a folder named “Luobi” containing your backup files. Luobi cannot see any other files in your Drive. Your Google account email is shown in Luobi’s Settings so you know which account is connected; it is not sent to us. You can disconnect at any time in Luobi’s Settings and revoke access in your Google Account.
  • Exported files. Backups, PDFs and share images you export are saved wherever you choose.

Google API Services

Luobi’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google Drive is used only to store and restore your own backups, is never transferred to anyone else, and is never used for advertising.

Permissions Luobi may ask for

  • Photos: Luobi uses the system photo picker, so it only receives the photos you choose. Saving a share image to your library requires “add photos” permission.
  • Face ID / passcode: used only to lock the app. Authentication is handled entirely by iOS; Luobi never receives biometric data.
  • Notifications: daily writing reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

Backup encryption

If you turn on backup encryption, your backups are encrypted on your device with a password that is stored only in your device’s Keychain. We cannot recover it.

Purchases

Luobi Pro purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment information.

Children

Luobi does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Questions? Email ut.eric.ma@gmail.com.

隐私政策

落笔（Luobi）· 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 19 日

落笔是由 Yuanqing Ma（以下称「我们」）开发的日记和笔记 App。简单来说：我们不收集、不保存、不出售，也看不到你的任何数据。你写下的所有内容都保存在你的设备上；如果你愿意，也可以同步到你自己的 iCloud 或你自己的 Google Drive。

我们收集的信息

没有。落笔没有账号系统，没有自己的服务器，没有统计分析，没有广告，也不做任何追踪。我们不会收到你的日记、笔记、照片、心情、标签或其他任何内容。

你的内容保存在哪里

  • 你的设备。所有日记、照片和设置都保存在你的 iPhone 上。
  • iCloud（可选，默认开启）。如果你已登录 iCloud，落笔会通过苹果的 CloudKit 私有数据库，在你自己的设备之间同步内容。这些数据存放在你的 iCloud 账户中，受苹果隐私政策约束，我们无法访问。你可以在落笔的设置里关闭 iCloud 同步。
  • Google Drive（可选，默认关闭）。如果你选择连接 Google Drive，落笔会通过 Google 登录申请 drive.file 权限。这个权限只允许落笔查看和管理它自己创建的文件，也就是一个名为「Luobi」、存放你备份文件的文件夹；落笔看不到你 Drive 里的任何其他文件。你的 Google 账号邮箱只会显示在落笔的设置里，方便你确认连接的是哪个账号，不会发送给我们。你可以随时在落笔的设置里断开，也可以在 Google 账号中撤销授权。
  • 导出的文件。你导出的备份、PDF 和分享图片，保存在你自己选择的位置。

Google API 服务

落笔对 Google API 所获信息的使用和传输，遵守 Google API 服务用户数据政策，包括其中的「有限使用」要求。来自 Google Drive 的数据只用于保存和恢复你自己的备份，不会转给任何人，也不会用于广告。

落笔可能请求的权限

  • 照片：落笔使用系统的照片选择器，只会拿到你亲自选中的照片。把分享图片存到相册时，需要「添加照片」权限。
  • Face ID / 锁屏密码：只用于给 App 上锁。验证完全由 iOS 完成，落笔不会接触到任何生物识别数据。
  • 通知：每日写日记提醒是在你的设备上本地安排的。

备份加密

如果你开启了备份加密，备份会在你的设备上用密码加密，这个密码只保存在你设备的钥匙串里，我们无法帮你找回。

购买

落笔 Pro 的购买和订阅由苹果通过 App Store 处理，我们不会收到你的支付信息。

儿童

落笔不向任何人（包括儿童）收集个人信息。

政策变更

如果本政策有变化，我们会更新本页面及上方的生效日期。
