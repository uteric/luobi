How do I sync between my iPhone and iPad?

Sign in to the same Apple Account on both devices and keep “iCloud Sync” on in Luobi’s Settings. Make sure Luobi is allowed in iPhone Settings › your name › iCloud, and that your iCloud storage isn’t full.

How do I back up to Google Drive?

Go to Settings › Google Drive Sync › Connect Google Drive. Luobi only accesses the “Luobi” folder it creates. You can also export a backup file at any time in Settings › Back Up to a File.

How do I move my entries to a new phone?

With iCloud Sync on, entries appear automatically after you sign in. Otherwise, restore from Google Drive or from an exported backup file.

I forgot my backup password.

The password is stored only on your device, so we can’t recover it. Unencrypted backups and iCloud sync are not affected.

How do I restore my Luobi Pro purchase?

Open Settings › Upgrade to Luobi Pro › Restore Purchases, using the same Apple Account you bought with. To manage or cancel a subscription, go to iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

怎样在 iPhone 和 iPad 之间同步？

两台设备登录同一个 Apple 账号，并在落笔的设置里保持「iCloud 同步」开启。同时确认 iPhone 设置 › 你的名字 › iCloud 里允许落笔使用 iCloud，并且 iCloud 空间没有用满。

怎样备份到 Google Drive？

打开 设置 › Google Drive 同步 › 连接 Google Drive。落笔只会访问它自己创建的「Luobi」文件夹。你也可以随时在 设置 › 备份到文件 里导出备份文件。

换新手机后怎么把日记搬过去？

开启了 iCloud 同步的话，在新手机登录同一个 Apple 账号后，日记会自动出现。否则可以从 Google Drive 或导出的备份文件恢复。

忘记了备份密码怎么办？

备份密码只保存在你的设备上，我们无法帮你找回。不加密的备份和 iCloud 同步不受影响。

怎样恢复落笔 Pro 的购买？

用购买时的 Apple 账号，打开 设置 › 升级到落笔 Pro › 恢复购买。管理或取消订阅，请到 iPhone 设置 › 你的名字 › 订阅。
